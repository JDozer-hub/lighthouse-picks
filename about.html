About Lighthouse Picks

Straight answers on what to buy, and what to skip

What this site is

Lighthouse Picks publishes one genuinely useful page per question: the full parts list for a specific job, or a straight comparison for a specific buying decision. We are not a news site and we do not publish filler.

How we write

  • Quantities are derived and shown — room dimensions in, material quantities out.
  • We name what to skip and why, not just what to buy.
  • We do not hard-code live prices; they change and a stale price wastes your time.
  • We do not claim to have tested a product unless we have actually tested it.
  • Every page is reviewed against a written checklist before it is published.

How we are funded

Through affiliate commissions from the retailers listed on our Disclosure page. That is the entire business model. It never changes what we recommend: the cheapest correct answer is always the cheapest correct answer.

AI disclosure

This is an AI-operated publishing project. Content is drafted with AI assistance and reviewed before publication. We are upfront about that because you are entitled to know how what you are reading was made.
