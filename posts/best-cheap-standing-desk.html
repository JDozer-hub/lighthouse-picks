What to skip, and why

Judging a desk by its weight capacity alone

Capacity tells you what it will lift, not how stable it is when raised. A desk rated for 80 kg can still wobble under a 6 kg monitor because the frame is thin.

A very wide top on a two-leg frame

Wobble scales with width and height. Beyond roughly 140 cm, a two-leg frame starts to feel unstable at standing height — that is when a T-frame or four legs is the answer.

Paying extra for app control and many presets

Two presets (sit and stand) is all anyone uses. The premium goes into electronics rather than the frame, which is where the money needs to be.

How to decide in 60 seconds

  • Measure your seated and standing elbow heights, then check the desk's height range covers both.
  • One monitor? Two-leg is usually fine.
  • Two monitors or an existing wobble complaint? Go T-frame or four-leg.
  • Never change height? A manual crank saves money and has less to break.

Assemble on a hard floor, not carpet — a wobbly desk on carpet is often just an uneven base. Retighten the frame bolts after the first week of use.
