What to skip, and why

A 200-piece accessory bit set bundled with the drill

It inflates the price with bits that will not fit a 10 mm chuck. Buy the drill bare or with one battery, then buy the four bits you actually use.

An 18V combi when you only drill plasterboard

You pay for torque you will never use and carry roughly double the weight. A 12V driver does flat jobs better, not worse.

Buying into a battery platform you cannot re-buy cheaply

The bare tool is not the cost — the batteries are. Check that spare batteries are affordable and available before you commit to a platform.

How to decide in 60 seconds

  • Drilling plasterboard and wood only? 12V brushless driver.
  • Drilling brick or concrete? 18V combi, or 18V plus an SDS if you do it often.
  • Drilling four times a year? Corded.
  • Already own batteries? Buy the bare tool on that platform.
