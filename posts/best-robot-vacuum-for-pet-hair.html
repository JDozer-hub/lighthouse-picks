What to skip, and why

A bristle brush in a multi-pet home

Hair wraps the bristles within a couple of runs, the roller stalls, and the machine reports a blockage. Rubber rollers resist this far better — it is the single most important spec for pet hair.

A self-emptying dock in a tight space

The dock is considerably bigger than a charging base and needs clearance. Measure the wall before buying, because the dock is what has to fit, not the robot.

Buying on suction figures alone

Suction is measured in conditions you will never reproduce, and airflow plus brush design is what actually lifts hair off carpet. It is a poor basis for comparison.

How to decide in 60 seconds

  • Heavy shedding on carpet? Rubber dual-roller, whatever else you compromise on.
  • Hard floors only and a low budget? A bristle model is honestly fine.
  • Will you empty the bin every day? If not, budget for a self-emptying dock.
  • Measure the dock footprint and the wall clearance before you order.

Robot vacuums are a maintenance commitment, not an appliance you forget. Budget a few minutes a month for cleaning the brush and sensors regardless of the model.
