What to skip, and why

A variable-temperature kettle with poor hold time

If it drops back to room temperature in ten minutes, you will re-boil constantly. Check the keep-warm duration rather than the number of preset buttons.

A plastic-bodied kettle for frequent tea

Plastic can retain flavours, particularly if you also boil water for other uses. Glass or stainless steel is the safer long-term choice.

Paying for temperature control when you only drink black tea

Black tea wants water at or near boiling. You would be paying for a feature you never use.

How to decide in 60 seconds

  • Only boiling water? Basic kettle — save the money.
  • Green, white or oolong tea? Variable temperature is genuinely worth it.
  • Pour-over coffee too? Gooseneck with variable temperature.
  • Check capacity against how many cups you make in one go — 1.7 L is the common default.

Temperature is the most useful variable in tea brewing, ahead of brand and ahead of brewing vessel. A cheap variable kettle beats an expensive fixed one for anything but black tea.
