What to skip, and why

A 'racing style' gaming chair for desk work

The bolsters push your shoulders forward and the shape is designed for a reclined driving position, not upright desk work. They look supportive and mostly are not.

Buying on weight rating alone

Weight rating says nothing about whether the chair fits you. A chair rated for far more than you weigh can still have a seat too deep or a lumbar pad in the wrong place.

Paying extra for a massage function or speakers

Every feature that is not ergonomic adjustment comes out of the budget for the parts you actually sit on. Under $200, spend the money on the mechanism.

How to decide in 60 seconds

  • Measure your knee height from the floor, then check the chair's seat height range covers it.
  • Check whether seat depth is adjustable — if not, you need a chair that matches your leg length exactly.
  • Mesh if you run warm, upholstered if you run cold.
  • If your pain is from sitting itself, consider a kneeling stool rather than another chair.

A second-hand commercial office chair is often better value than a new budget chair, because the mechanism and gas lift are built for daily use. Check the gas lift holds before buying.
